## PCD TSUNAMI research data

The [dataset authors](https://sakuradaken.net/pcd_dataset.html) retain copyright in PCD TSUNAMI
and permit research use with citation of:

Ken Sakurada and Takayuki Okatani. *Change Detection from a Street Image Pair using CNN Features
and Superpixel Segmentation*. British Machine Vision Conference, 2015.

Raw photographs, masks, the dataset archive, and checkpoints trained using this dataset are
**not distributed** in this repository or its release assets. The reproduction script downloads
the archive directly from the authors' linked distribution. Its use is subject to their terms.
The original-code notice supplies no dataset or checkpoint permissions. Contact the dataset
owners before redistributing data, distributing learned weights, or pursuing use outside the
permitted research scope. This is a conservative publication boundary, not a determination that
all learned parameters are copyrighted derivatives.

Committed reports contain experiment statistics, split identifiers, provenance, and checksums.
The cached policy arrays contain procedural-scene measurements and features, not PCD images.
The [dataset card](docs/dataset-card.md) documents the protocol and limitations.
